READING COMFORT UPDATE

Open the app normally, then select the “Aa Reading comfort” button in the lower-right corner.

Included features:
- Cream, pale yellow, peach, pink, green, aqua, blue, grey and lavender colour presets
- Custom colour picker and filter-strength control
- Font, text size, letter spacing and line spacing controls
- Brightness, contrast, dark mode and reduced-glare controls
- Draggable reading ruler highlighting 1 to 5 lines
- One-tap removal of all reading changes
- Four anonymous numbered profiles stored locally in the browser
- Current settings restored automatically on the same browser/device

No student name is attached to a reading profile, and settings are not transmitted anywhere.

The added feature is contained mainly in:
- reading-comfort.js
- the Reading Comfort section at the end of styles.css
- the Reading Comfort interface near the end of index.html

The service-worker cache version was increased so deployed devices retrieve the updated files.
